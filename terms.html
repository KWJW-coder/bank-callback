Terms of use – MyFamilyExpenses

MyFamilyExpenses is a private, non-commercial household budgeting app. It is used only by the developer and invited family members to view their own bank account information.

  • The app only reads account information; it does not initiate payments.
  • Only accounts belonging to the user may be connected.
  • The app is provided as is, without warranty. Figures shown may differ from official bank statements.
  • Use of the Enable Banking API is subject to Enable Banking's own terms for restricted applications.
