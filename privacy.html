Privacy policy – MyFamilyExpenses

MyFamilyExpenses is a personal household budgeting app used privately by its developer and family members. It is not distributed to the public.

What data is used

With the explicit consent of the account holder, the app reads account information and transactions of the account holder's own bank accounts through the Enable Banking API (PSD2 account information service).

How it is stored

Transactions are stored only in the app on the user's own devices and in the user's own iCloud container. The API credentials are kept in the device Keychain. There is no developer-operated server, and no data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Consent and deletion

Bank access can be withdrawn at any time with the bank, and expires automatically after the consent period. Data can be deleted by removing it in the app or deleting the app.
